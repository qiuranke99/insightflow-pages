iCloud Sync

Make sure iCloud Drive is enabled for your Apple Account and that Insight Flow has iCloud access enabled in system settings before turning on sync in the app. Sync covers text notes, tags, links, and voice transcripts. Image attachments and voice audio files stay on the device where they were added.

Permissions

Camera, Photo Library, Microphone, Speech Recognition, and Face ID are only requested when you use related features. Speech audio may be sent to Apple for transcription.

Export

Exports are generated on-device. Depending on your note library size, JSON or Markdown export may take a short time to prepare.

Trash Recovery

Deleted notes move to Trash first. You can restore them from Trash until you permanently delete them or empty Trash.
